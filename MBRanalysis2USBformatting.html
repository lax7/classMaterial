Section 3: USB Formatting Analysis (FTK Imager)

In this activity, you will use **FTK Imager** to see how data persists or is destroyed depending on the format method used.

Pre-Lab Setup: Before starting, insert a USB drive and copy at least one recognizable file (e.g., a Word document or a JPG image) onto it.

Step 1: Quick Format vs. Full Format Comparison

  1. Quick Format: With your file already on the drive, perform a "Quick Format" (FAT32 or NTFS). Open the drive in FTK Imager (File > Add Evidence Item > Physical Drive). Explore the sectors. Can you still find the hex data of your document?
  2. Full Format: Copy the file back onto the drive. Now, perform a "Full Format" (this will take longer). Refresh FTK Imager and inspect the sectors again.
