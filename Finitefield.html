Finite Field (Galois) Existence

A finite field (also called Galois field) with order(cardinality) m only exists if m is a prime power, i.e., m = pn, for some positive integer n and prime integer p. p is called the characteristic of the finite field.

Examples of Valid Orders (m = pn)

These sizes allow for the construction of a field (GF(m)).
