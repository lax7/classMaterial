Presentation Rules & Outline

⏱️Time Limit: 6 Minutes Total. If you go beyond 6 minutes, 10 points will be deducted.

⚠️ Presentation Grading:

Your grades will be based on the performance on Part 1 — Part 4.

Part 1: Context (1 Slide / ~1 Mins)
Slide 1: Intro. Creat a Image/visual that explain your whole project. (no bullets allowed)
Part 2: The "Deep Dive" (Live Environment / ~3 Mins)
  • No slides.
  • The goal is to show your completed project in the best way possible.
  • Switch to your Live Environment. Show data sources (CSV, Database, API), Virtual Machines, or code logic.
  • Show the "brain" of the project.
  • Use a Live Demo or a Pre-recorded Video to showcase functionality.
  • Get everything ready beforehand. Have all code, tabs, and assets loaded before you step up.
  • Do not waste time on technical setups.
Part 3: Conclusion (1 Slide ~ 1 min)
What is your conclusion? Summarize what you learned that you didn't know at the start. If you could redo, what would you do differently.
Part 4: Question and Answer ( ~ 1 min)
