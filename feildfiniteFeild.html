Field & Finite Field Exercises

A Field is a set with two operations (+ and ×) that satisfies all 11 axioms of arithmetic:

1. Additive Abelian Group: Closure, Assoc, Identity(0), Inverse(-), Commutative.
2. Multiplicative Abelian Group (Excluding 0): Closure, Assoc, Identity(1), Inverse(1/x), Commutative.
3. Distributivity: a(b + c) = ab + ac.

1. Rational Numbers (ℚ)

All numbers that can be written as a fraction p/q.

RequirementStatusReasoning
Abelian Group (+)Passed0 is a fraction; every fraction has a negative.
Abelian Group (×)Passed1 is a fraction; the inverse of p/q is q/p (both are fractions).
DistributivityPassedHolds for all rational arithmetic.
RESULT: This is an INFINITE FIELD.

2. Integers (ℤ)

Whole numbers {..., -1, 0, 1, ...}

RequirementStatusReasoning
Abelian Group (+)PassedStandard integer addition properties.
Multiplicative InverseFailedThe inverse of 2 is 1/2, which is NOT an integer.
RESULT: This is a RING, but NOT a Field.

3. Finite Field Modulo 5 (ℤ₅)

Set: {0, 1, 2, 3, 4} | Operations: Modulo 5 Addition & Multiplication.

RequirementStatusReasoning
Abelian Group (+)PassedForms a complete additive group mod 5.
Multiplicative InversePassedEvery non-zero element has an inverse: 1×1=1, 2×3=1, 4×4=1 (mod 5).
RESULT: This is a FINITE FIELD (Galois Field GF(5)).

4. Integers Modulo 4 (ℤ₄)

Set: {0, 1, 2, 3} | Operations: Modulo 4 Addition & Multiplication.

RequirementStatusReasoning
Abelian Group (+)PassedForms a group mod 4.
Multiplicative InverseFailed2 × 1 = 2, 2 × 2 = 0, 2 × 3 = 2. 2 has no inverse that results in 1.
RESULT: NOT a Field (Modulo must be prime to be a field).
