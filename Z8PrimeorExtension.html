Exploring 2^3=8, Prime (Z_8) vs Extension Field GF(2^3)

1. Why Standard Integers Modulo 8 Fail

If we use 8 as a simple modulus, 2, 4, and 6 have no inverses. They are "trapped" because they share factors with 8.

2. Mapping Elements to Polynomials

In extension field, we view these 8 elements as polynomials.

0: 0
1: 1
2: x
3: x + 1
4: x2
5: x2 + 1
6: x2 + x
7: x2 + x + 1
