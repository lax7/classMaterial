Multiplicative Group Exercises

For a set S under multiplication (×) to be a group, it must satisfy:

1. Closure: a × b ∈ S.
2. Associativity: (a × b) × c = a × (b × c).
3. Identity: There is an e ∈ S where a × e = a (For multiplication, e = 1).
4. Inverse: For every a, there is a 1/a ∈ S where a × (1/a) = 1.
5. Commutativity: a × b = b × a.

1. All Non-Zero Rational Numbers (Q \ {0})

Set: All fractions except zero | Operation: Multiplication

ConditionStatusReasoning
ClosurePassedMultiplying two non-zero fractions always results in a non-zero fraction.
AssociativityPassedMultiplication of numbers is associative.
IdentityPassedThe number 1 is a rational number.
InversePassedThe inverse of p/q is q/p. Since we excluded zero, p is never 0.
CommutativityPassed(1/2) * (2/3) = (2/3) * (1/2).
RESULT: This is a Multiplicative Abelian Group.

2. Integers {..., -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, ...}

Set: ℤ | Operation: Multiplication

ConditionStatusReasoning
ClosurePassedInteger × Integer = Integer.
AssociativityPassedStandard property of multiplication.
IdentityPassed1 is an integer.
InverseFailedThe inverse of 2 is 1/2. 1/2 is NOT an integer.
CommutativityPasseda * b = b * a.
RESULT: This is NOT Multiplicative Abelian Group.

3. The Set {1, -1}

Set: S = {1, -1} | Operation: Multiplication

ConditionStatusReasoning
ClosurePassed1*1=1, (-1)*(-1)=1, 1*(-1)=-1. All results are in the set.
AssociativityPassedStandard property.
IdentityPassed1 is in the set.
InversePassedThe inverse of 1 is 1. The inverse of -1 is -1 (because -1 * -1 = 1).
CommutativityPassedOrder does not matter.
RESULT: This IS a Multiplicative Abelian Group.

4. Non-Zero Integers Modulo 5 {0, 1, 2, 3, 4}

Set: U(5) | Operation: Multiplication Modulo 5

In this system, 2 × 3 = 6. Since 6 ÷ 5 leaves a remainder of 1, 2 × 3 = 1.

ConditionStatusReasoning
ClosurePassedAll products mod 5 (1×1=1, 2×4=3, 3×4=2, etc.) stay within {1, 2, 3, 4}.
AssociativityPassedModular multiplication is associative.
IdentityPassed1 is the identity.
InversePassed1→1, 2→3, 3→2, 4→4. Every element has a partner (inverse) such that a and inverse a= 1 except for element 0
CommutativityPassed2 * 4 mod 5 = 3 and 4 * 2 mod 5 = 3.
RESULT: This IS a Mulitiplicative Abelian Group.
